Step	Description


1 Log in to Zapier.
2 Create a new Zap.
3 Select Gmail as the Trigger App.
4 Choose Trigger Event: New Email Matching Search.
5 Search Filter: subject:Assignment.
6 Select Google Drive as the Action App.
7 Choose Action Event: Upload File.
8 Select the destination folder in Google Drive.
9 Turn ON the Zap

Result

Whenever an email with subject Assignment arrives, Zapier automatically uploads its attachment to Google Drive.
